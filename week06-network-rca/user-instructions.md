Step 1: Check your internet connection

* Open your web browser.
* Go to a website, such as www.google.com.
* If the website loads: Your home Wi-Fi and internet are working properly, but your laptop cannot find the ACME file system. You will want to restart your company provided VPN.
* If the website does not load, that means your internet connection is offline. Check your Wi-Fi settings or restart your internet router before proceeding.



Step 2: Restart your network connection

* Click the Wi-Fi icon on your taskbar.
* Turn Wi-Fi off, wait about 10 seconds, and turn it back on.
* If you are using a network cable plugged into your laptop, Unplug the cable, wait about 10 seconds, and plug it back in.
* Restart your laptop.

When to stop and contact the Helpdesk



Please contact the ACME Helpdesk if you experience any of the following:

* You complete all the steps above, but you still cannot open ACME resources.
* You see a yellow warning triangle or red "X" icon over your network icon that does not disappear after restarting.
* Your VPN application gives an error on startup
